# The World Today — Thursday 19 November 2026

A dense broadsheet front page summing up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026: GTA VI launches worldwide to a 99 on Metacritic, Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first-ever trilateral talks at the APEC summit in Shenzhen, and the International Court of Justice delivers its verdict in the Rohingya genocide case against Myanmar — plus thirty more verified stories from every region, from the record Anthropic IPO on Nasdaq to Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India's east coast. The image is an original generated broadsheet; `main.py` simply copies it to the output directory.
